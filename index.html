03 / Learning notes

Small questions worth exploring.

What does “fast” really measure?

A networked application has several clocks: connection setup, data delivery, processing and display. Understanding a measurement starts with identifying exactly where its clock begins and ends.

A question about measurement

What makes a useful prediction?

A model's score only becomes meaningful alongside the data split, a sensible baseline and the consequences of its errors. I am interested in evaluation that explains behaviour as well as summarising it.

A question about evaluation

When is more machinery worthwhile?

Splitting a job across workers adds coordination and data movement. Exploring the balance between useful computation and that extra work is one of the reasons distributed computing interests me.

A question about scale

Build something small.
Understand it well.
Then ask the next question.
